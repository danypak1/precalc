What a question looks like

Solve \(|3x-6| > 9\). Which is the solution set?

A(−1, 5)
B(−∞, −1) ∪ (5, ∞)
C(5, ∞)
DNo solution
✗ Not quite — the answer is (B) What this mistake means: you split the absolute value the way a less-than is split. Greater-than asks which points are far from 2, and far in either direction: 3x − 6 > 9 or 3x − 6 < −9. Only a less-than traps x between two numbers and gives one interval. Test x = 0: |−6| = 6, which is not more than 9 — so 0 cannot be in the answer, and (A) contains it.
